# AquaPure Model A2 Water Filter - Quick Manual

> SAMPLE DATA: "AquaPure A2" is a fictional product invented for this demo.

## Installing the cartridge
Turn off the water supply, twist the housing counter-clockwise, insert the new cartridge, and twist the housing back until it clicks. Run water through the filter for 5 minutes before drinking.

## Replacing the cartridge
Replace the cartridge every 6 months or after 1,500 litres, whichever comes first. The red indicator light turns on when replacement is due.

## Troubleshooting
If the flow is weak, check that the cartridge is seated correctly and that the water supply valve is fully open. If the red light stays on after replacing the cartridge, hold the reset button for 5 seconds.

## Warranty
The filter housing has a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Cartridges are consumables and are not covered.
