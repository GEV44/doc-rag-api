# Brightline Outdoor Gear - Returns Policy

> SAMPLE DATA: "Brightline Outdoor Gear" is a fictional shop invented for this demo. Not a real policy.

## Return window
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with tags attached.

## Non-returnable items
Opened sleeping bags, swimwear, and personalised products cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the parcel. Original shipping costs are not refunded unless the return is due to our error.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact support within 7 days with a photo. We will send a replacement or refund at no cost, including return shipping.

## Exchanges
To exchange for a different size or colour, place a new order and return the original item. We refund the original order once the parcel arrives.
