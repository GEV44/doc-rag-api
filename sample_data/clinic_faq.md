# Willow Creek Family Clinic - FAQ

> SAMPLE DATA: "Willow Creek Family Clinic" is a fictional organisation invented for this demo. Not real information.

## Opening hours
The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00, and on Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Booking and cancelling appointments
Appointments can be booked by phone or through the online portal. Please cancel at least 24 hours in advance so that another patient can use the slot. Late cancellations may be charged a missed-appointment fee.

## What to bring
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of the medications you currently take. New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to complete registration forms.

## Test results
Routine blood test results are usually available within 3 working days. You will receive a message in the patient portal when they are ready. Results are never given out by e-mail.

## Parking
Free parking for patients is available behind the building. Disabled bays are next to the main entrance.
