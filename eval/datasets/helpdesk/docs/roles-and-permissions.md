# Roles and Permissions

## Workspace roles
There are four roles: Owner, Admin, Member and Guest. Owners control billing and can delete the workspace. Admins manage members and projects. Members create and edit tasks. Guests can only see projects they were invited to.

## Inviting people
Admins invite people by email from the Members page. An invitation expires after 7 days and can be sent again. Guests are free on the Team and Business plans, up to 10 per workspace.

## Project privacy
A private project is visible only to people added to it. Admins can see that a private project exists, but cannot read its tasks unless they join it.

## Transferring ownership
The current Owner can hand the workspace to another Admin under Settings, then Members. The previous Owner becomes an Admin automatically.
