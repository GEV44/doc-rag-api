# Mobile App

## Supported devices
The Tallyhive mobile app runs on iOS 16 or later and Android 11 or later. A tablet layout is available on iPad but not yet on Android tablets.

## Offline mode
Tasks you have opened recently are available offline. Edits made without a connection are saved on the phone and uploaded automatically when the device reconnects. If two people edit the same field, the most recent edit wins.

## Widgets
The home screen widget shows tasks due today. It refreshes about every 30 minutes and cannot be used to complete a task.

## Biometric lock
You can protect the app with Face ID, a fingerprint or a PIN. The lock engages after one minute in the background by default.
