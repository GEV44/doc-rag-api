# Account and Login

## Resetting your password
Open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". Tallyhive emails a reset link that stays valid for 45 minutes. If the email does not arrive within five minutes, check your spam folder before requesting another link.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication can be switched on under Settings, then Security. Tallyhive supports authenticator apps and printed backup codes. SMS codes are not offered. Each account receives ten backup codes, and each code works only once.

## Locked accounts
After six failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. Workspace owners can unlock a member immediately from the Members page.

## Changing your email address
Go to Settings, then Profile, and enter the new address. A confirmation link is sent to the new address and the change takes effect only after you click it.
