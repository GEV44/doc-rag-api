# Billing and Plans

## Plans
Tallyhive has three plans: Free, Team and Business. The Free plan allows up to 3 members and 5 projects. The Team plan allows up to 50 members and unlimited projects. The Business plan adds single sign-on and audit logs.

## Billing cycle
Paid plans are billed monthly or annually. Choosing annual billing gives two months free compared with paying monthly. Invoices are issued on the same calendar day each period.

## Refunds
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Bank transfer is available only on the Business plan with annual billing. Invoices can be sent to a separate billing contact under Settings, then Billing.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, Tallyhive retries it three times over seven days. The workspace turns read-only if all retries fail, and returns to normal as soon as payment succeeds.
