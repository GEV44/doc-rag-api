# Notifications

## Email digests
By default Tallyhive sends one email digest each morning at 8:00 in your local time. You can switch the digest to weekly or turn it off under Settings, then Notifications.

## Mentions
Typing @ followed by a name notifies that person immediately, even when their digest is turned off. Mentions in a private project reach only members of that project.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours pause push notifications between times you choose. Notifications that arrive during quiet hours are delivered together when the period ends.

## Muting a project
Muting a project stops all notifications from it, except direct mentions. Muted projects are shown in grey in the sidebar.
