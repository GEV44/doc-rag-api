# Data Export and Privacy

## Exporting your data
Workspace owners can export all tasks and comments as a ZIP file containing JSON and CSV from Settings, then Data. Large workspaces may take up to 24 hours, and the download link is emailed to the owner.

## Data retention
Deleted tasks stay in the Trash for 30 days and can be restored. After that they are permanently erased from our systems. Backups are overwritten within 90 days.

## Data location
Workspaces are stored in the EU region by default. Business plan customers can choose the US region at creation time. The region cannot be changed afterwards.

## Closing an account
Closing a workspace deletes its data after a 14 day grace period. During the grace period the owner can reopen the workspace by signing in.
