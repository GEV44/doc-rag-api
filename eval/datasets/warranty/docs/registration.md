# Product Registration

## Why register
Registering your appliance links the invoice to the serial number. It makes warranty claims faster and lets us contact you about safety notices.

## How to register
Register on the Halden website within 60 days of purchase. You need the serial number from the rating plate and a photo of the invoice. Registration takes about two minutes.

## Registration bonus
Customers who register within 60 days get an extra year of warranty on the control board of their appliance. The bonus is applied automatically.

## Transferring ownership
If you sell an appliance, the new owner can take over the warranty. Send the new owner's details and the original invoice to customer service.
