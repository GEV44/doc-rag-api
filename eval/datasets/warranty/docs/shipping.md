# Delivery and Installation

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. Large appliances are delivered by a two-person team on a date agreed by phone. Delivery to islands and remote areas can take up to 10 working days.

## Delivery costs
Delivery is free for orders over 300 euros. Below that amount, delivery costs 29 euros.

## Checking on arrival
Check the appliance for visible damage before signing the delivery note. Damage found later than 48 hours after delivery may not be accepted as a transport claim.

## Installation service
Halden can install washing machines, dishwashers and ovens for 79 euros. Gas connections are always made by a certified installer and are not part of this service.

## Old appliance removal
We take away your old appliance for 25 euros when we deliver the new one.
