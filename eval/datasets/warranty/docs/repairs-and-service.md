# Repairs and Service

## Requesting a repair
Open a service request on the Halden website or call the service line. Have your model number and serial number ready. You will receive a case number by email.

## Technician visits
A technician visit is normally scheduled within five working days of the request. Visits take place in a four-hour window, and someone over 18 must be at home.

## Repair costs
Repairs under warranty are free of charge, including parts and labour. For appliances out of warranty, the callout fee is 49 euros, and parts are charged at list price.

## Repair time
Most repairs are completed during the first visit. If a part has to be ordered, it usually arrives in 7 to 10 working days.

## Replacement
If the same fault cannot be fixed after three repair attempts, Halden will replace the appliance with an equivalent model.

## Spare parts
Spare parts for each model remain available for at least ten years after the model is discontinued.
