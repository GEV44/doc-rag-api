# Safety Notices and Recalls

## How we announce recalls
Safety recalls are published on the Halden website and emailed to every registered owner. Affected models are listed with their serial number range.

## What to do
Stop using an appliance that is subject to a recall and unplug it. Contact customer service with your serial number to arrange a free repair or a replacement.

## Costs
Recall repairs and replacements are free of charge, even when the appliance is out of warranty. No proof of purchase is needed if the serial number is in the affected range.

## Reporting a safety problem
If an appliance overheats, sparks or smells of burning, unplug it and report it using the safety form. A safety specialist will respond within 24 hours.
