# Halden Care Plans

## Care Plus
Care Plus extends the warranty from two to five years and costs 89 euros for small appliances and 149 euros for large ones. It must be purchased within 30 days of buying the appliance.

## What Care Plus adds
The plan includes one free annual service visit and covers parts that wear out through normal use, such as pumps and heating elements. Consumable parts are still excluded.

## Care Plus Premium
Care Plus Premium adds accidental damage cover, for example a cracked glass door. A deductible of 50 euros applies for each claim, and a maximum of two claims are allowed per year.

## Cancelling a plan
A care plan can be cancelled within 30 days for a full refund. After that, a prorated refund is given for the remaining full months.
