# Returns and Cancellations

## Return window
You can return an appliance bought from the Halden online shop within 30 days of delivery for a refund. The appliance must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Return shipping
Halden collects returned appliances from your address free of charge for items over 20 kilograms. Smaller items must be posted back at the customer's expense, unless they arrived damaged.

## Refund timing
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 working days after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection.

## Cancelling an order
An order can be cancelled free of charge before it ships. After shipping, the normal return process applies.

## Opened or installed items
Built-in appliances that have been installed cannot be returned for change of mind, but they remain covered by the warranty.
