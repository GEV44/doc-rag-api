# Warranty Coverage

## Standard warranty
Every Halden appliance comes with a two-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. The motor of a washing machine or dryer is covered for ten years.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, power surges, or repairs by anyone other than an authorised service partner. Cosmetic damage such as scratches and dents is also excluded.

## Consumable parts
Filters, door seals, light bulbs and drawer handles are consumable parts. They are covered for the first 90 days only.

## Commercial use
Appliances used in a business, such as a cafe or a rental flat, have a reduced warranty of one year.

## Proof of purchase
To claim under warranty you need the original invoice or the registration confirmation. Without either, the warranty period is counted from the production date printed on the rating plate.
