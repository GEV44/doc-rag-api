# Code of Conduct

## Respectful workplace
Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated in any form, in the office or online. This applies to colleagues, contractors, candidates and visitors.

## Reporting concerns
Concerns can be reported to a manager, to the People team, or anonymously through the Speak Up line. Reports are acknowledged within two working days. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

## Conflicts of interest
Employees must disclose any financial interest in a supplier, competitor or customer. Outside work for another company needs written approval before it starts.

## Gifts
Gifts worth more than 50 euros must be declined or handed to the People team. Cash or cash equivalents may never be accepted.

## Confidential information
Customer data and unreleased product plans must not be shared outside the company. This duty continues after employment ends.
