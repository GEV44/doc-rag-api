# Travel and Expenses

## Booking travel
Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is used for flights shorter than six hours. Business class may be approved for longer flights by a director.

## Daily allowance
Employees on business trips receive a meal allowance of 45 euros per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Hotel costs are covered up to 150 euros per night, or 220 euros in major cities such as London and New York.

## Submitting expenses
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase through the Finance app. Each claim needs a receipt for any amount over 25 euros. Claims submitted late may be refused.

## Reimbursement timing
Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll. Claims approved after the 20th are paid one month later.

## Company cards
Managers and frequent travellers can request a company card. Cards must not be used for personal purchases.
