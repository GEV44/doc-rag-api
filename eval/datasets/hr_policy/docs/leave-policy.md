# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees at Larkspur Labs receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, and new hires receive a prorated amount for their first year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into March of the next year.

## Sick leave
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days each year. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive sick days. Sick days do not carry over.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The second parent receives 6 weeks. Leave can be taken at any point during the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## Public holidays
Larkspur Labs observes the public holidays of the country where the employee is based. A holiday that falls on a weekend is not replaced by a day off.

## Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days can be approved by a manager for personal reasons. Requests longer than 30 days need approval from the People team.
