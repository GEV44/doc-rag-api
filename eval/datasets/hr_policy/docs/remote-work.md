# Remote Work Policy

## Working location
Employees may work remotely up to three days a week. Teams agree on shared office days so that everyone overlaps at least twice a week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department head.

## Working abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per year. Longer stays need approval from People and Finance because of tax and legal rules. Work must not be done from a country on the restricted list.

## Home office stipend
New employees get a one-time stipend of 600 euros for a desk, chair or monitor. The stipend is paid with the first salary and does not need receipts. A further 40 euros per month covers internet costs.

## Core hours
Everyone is expected to be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 in their team's main time zone. Meetings should not be scheduled outside these hours without agreement.
