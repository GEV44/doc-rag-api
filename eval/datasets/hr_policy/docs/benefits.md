# Benefits

## Health insurance
The company pays the full premium for private health insurance for the employee and 50 percent for family members. Cover starts on the first day of employment.

## Pension
Larkspur Labs matches pension contributions up to 5 percent of salary. Matching begins after probation ends.

## Learning budget
Every employee has a learning budget of 1,200 euros per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel is paid from the expense policy, not from the learning budget.

## Wellbeing
A wellbeing allowance of 30 euros per month can be used for a gym, a sports club or a meditation app. Employees also get access to a confidential counselling service, free of charge for up to six sessions a year.

## Equity
Employees who stay for at least one year are eligible for the share option plan. Options vest over four years with a one-year cliff.
