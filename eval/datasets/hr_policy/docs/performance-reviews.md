# Performance Reviews

## Review cycle
Larkspur Labs runs reviews twice a year, in June and in December. Each review combines a self-assessment, feedback from three colleagues and the manager's assessment.

## Rating scale
Ratings use five levels, from Needs Improvement to Outstanding. Ratings are discussed in calibration meetings so that managers apply the scale consistently.

## Promotions
Promotion decisions are made once a year in January. An employee needs to have shown the next level's skills for at least two review cycles. Promotions take effect on 1 April.

## Pay reviews
Salary changes follow the December review and are paid from February. Employees rated Needs Improvement are not eligible for a pay increase.

## Improvement plans
An employee who is rated Needs Improvement receives a written plan with goals for the following 90 days. Progress is checked every two weeks with the manager.
