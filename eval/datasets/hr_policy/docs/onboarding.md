# Onboarding

## First week
New employees receive their laptop one week before the start date. On day one they meet their manager and a buddy, who answers day-to-day questions for the first three months.

## Probation
Probation lasts six months. A review meeting is held at the end of the third month, and the manager confirms the employment at the end of the sixth month. Notice during probation is two weeks.

## Required training
Security awareness and data protection training must be completed within the first 30 days. Access to production systems is granted only after both are finished.

## Accounts and tools
IT creates the email, chat and calendar accounts before the first day. Requests for additional software go through the IT service desk and are usually answered within two working days.
